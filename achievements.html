Bootcamp on “Content Creation Automation Using Agents”

11 August 2026 Sri Sairam Engineering College

The Computational and Cognitive Intelligence Club (CCIC) of the Department of Computer Science & Engineering (Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning) at Sri Sairam Engineering College successfully organized a Bootcamp on “Content Creation Automation Using Agents” on 11 August 2026. The event focused on introducing participants to AI agents and their practical applications in learning, building, and automating content creation workflows. The event concluded with the recognition of outstanding participants, with the First Prize of ₹2,000, Second Prize of ₹1,000, and Third Prize of ₹500 awarded to the top three performers. Their achievements reflect the creativity, technical understanding, and practical application of AI concepts demonstrated during the event.
