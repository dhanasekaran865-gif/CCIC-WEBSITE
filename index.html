Our Institution

Sri Sai Ram Engineering College, Chennai

Sri Sairam Engineering College, Chennai, was established in the year 1995 by MJF.Ln.Leo Muthu, Chairman of Sapthagiri Educational Trust, as a non-profitable, and non-minority institution. A well-defined vision, highly committed mission, and dedicated leadership facilitate Sri Sairam Engineering College to be counted among the country’s premier educational institutions. Since its inception, our Institution has grown into a vast conglomerate of magnificent buildings, state-of-the-art and sophisticated laboratories, high-speed internet computing centers, a modern digital library, and a superlative sports complex – each a landmark across an expansive 300-acre green campus.

Its quiet and idyllic surroundings, comprising architecturally and aesthetically designed academic blocks, sports playgrounds, and lush greenery, make it one of the most preferred destinations for aspirants of Engineering studies. The College is affiliated with Anna University and approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi.

Today, Sri Sairam Engineering College has developed into a reputed Engineering Institution with ISO 9001:2008 certification and NBA Accreditation across engineering disciplines. Technical student chapters and specialized clubs function vibrantly with workshops, hackathons, guest lectures, and conferences. In this thriving environment, the Computational and Cognitive Intelligence Club (CCIC) was established under the Department of Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning to spearhead student research and technical mastery in agentic systems and modern AI.
