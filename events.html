SEC202610CCC03 IEEE / EMBS / IEEE Sairam Online (Google Meet)

COGNIX – AI Creativity & Cognitive Intelligence Challenge

CREATE. THINK. EXPLAIN.
Date: 12th October 2026
Time: 7:00 PM – 9:00 PM
Team Size: Max 2 Members
Interactive AI Quiz Poster Presentation Session

COGNIX, organized by the Computational and Cognitive Intelligence Club (CCIC) of the Department of Computer Science and Engineering (Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning), Sri Sairam Engineering College, provided students with an engaging platform to showcase their creativity, knowledge, and innovative thinking in Artificial Intelligence and Cognitive Intelligence.

The event featured two major activities: an interactive quiz that tested participants’ knowledge, logical reasoning, and understanding of AI-related concepts, and a poster presentation session, where students presented their creative ideas and explained their posters to demonstrate their understanding of the chosen topics.

Through the quiz, participants challenged their knowledge and critical-thinking abilities, while the poster explanations encouraged them to communicate ideas effectively, demonstrate originality, and explore the practical applications of AI in addressing real-world challenges.

COGNIX successfully encouraged active participation, collaborative learning, creative expression, and the exchange of innovative ideas, inspiring students to think critically and explore the evolving relationship between human cognition and Artificial Intelligence.

The Challenge Question:
“How does cognitive intelligence shape the way we think, learn, make decisions, and solve problems today—and how might it shape our future?”
Challenge Tracks:
01. The Future of Education 02. The Distracted Mind 03. Small Change, Big Impact 04. Human Mind × AI 05. Ideas into Impact 06. Cognitive Intelligence: Present & Future
